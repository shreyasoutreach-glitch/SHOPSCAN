# A11yForge outreach corpus

This corpus is a **prospecting starting point, not a claim that every domain currently has an accessibility defect**.

## Source
50 high-search Indian D2C/ecommerce prospects were taken from MerchantData's September 2026 Shopify leaderboard. The source ranks Shopify D2C brands by estimated monthly branded Google searches in India.

## Commercial sprint math
- 50 prospects loaded
- Tier A: 25 highest-search prospects
- Tier B: next 25
- First pass target: 25 verified scans
- Evidence target: 15 personalized audit artifacts
- Conversation target: 10 qualified conversations
- Pilot target: 5
- Paid target: 2
- Revenue target: INR 50,000+ initial paid pilots

These are **operating targets, not achieved results**. A11yForge should only move a prospect through the funnel when an actual scan, contact, reply, meeting, pilot or payment exists.

## Outreach wedge
Lead with a concrete verified observation from the merchant's rendered storefront. Do not claim WCAG certification, legal compliance or lawsuit protection.

Recommended loop:
1. Scan public storefront.
2. Verify the finding in Chromium at desktop/mobile.
3. Capture evidence and evidence-head hash.
4. Package one high-value finding plus remediation/regression proof.
5. Send a short personalized outreach message.
6. Track reply -> call -> pilot -> paid conversion.

## Qualification
Prioritize storefronts with:
- meaningful purchase/navigation flows,
- high search demand,
- visible dynamic UI,
- forms, product selectors, cart/checkout surfaces,
- agencies or ecommerce teams likely to own remediation.

## Data hygiene
Re-scan domains immediately before outreach. Store current scan status, verified finding count, candidate count, rendered status, overlay evidence and outreach state separately.
